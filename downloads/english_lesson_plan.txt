English Lesson Plan: Beginner Level

Topic: Greetings and Introductions

Vocabulary:
* Hello, Hi (Greetings)
* Goodbye, Bye (Farewells)
* My name is...
* What is your name?
* Nice to meet you.
* Thank you.
* You're welcome.

Grammar Focus:
* To be verb (I am, you are)

Example Dialogue:
A: Hello! My name is [Your Name]. What is your name?
B: Hi! My name is [Other Name]. Nice to meet you.
A: Nice to meet you too.
B: Goodbye!
A: Bye!
