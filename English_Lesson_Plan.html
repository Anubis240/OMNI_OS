1 Hour Beginner English Lesson Plan

This plan is designed for a complete beginner, focusing on fundamental communication skills.

1. Warm-up (10 minutes)

  • **Activity:** Greetings and Introductions
  • **Content:** Learn and practice basic greetings (Hello, Hi, Good morning/afternoon).
  • **Goal:** Students introduce themselves (e.g., "Hello, my name is [Name]").

2. Vocabulary (15 minutes)

  • **Activity:** Flashcards and Visual Aids
  • **Content:** Common objects (e.g., pen, book, table, chair) and basic verbs (e.g., eat, drink, read, write).
  • **Goal:** Recognize and pronounce essential everyday words.

3. Grammar (15 minutes)

  • **Topic:** The verb "to be"
  • **Content:** Introduction to "I am," "You are," "He/She/It is."
  • **Goal:** Construct simple, basic sentences using the verb "to be."

4. Activity (15 minutes)

  • **Activity:** Simple Role-Play
  • **Content:** Create a simple dialogue based on learned vocabulary and grammar (e.g., ordering coffee, simple shopping interaction, or asking for basic directions).
  • **Goal:** Apply learned concepts in a practical, communicative context.

5. Cool-down (5 minutes)

  • **Activity:** Review and Wrap-up
  • **Content:** Quick recap of key vocabulary and grammar points covered during the hour.
  • **Goal:** Consolidate learning and provide feedback.
