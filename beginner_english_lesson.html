1-Hour Beginner English Lesson

Section 1: Warm-up & Greetings (10 mins)

Start with simple greetings.

  • Hello, Hi
  • Good morning / afternoon / evening
  • How are you? - I'm fine, thank you.

Section 2: Basic Vocabulary (20 mins)

Focus on essential words.

  • Numbers (1-10): One, Two, Three, Four, Five, Six, Seven, Eight, Nine, Ten
  • Colors: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Black, White
  • Common Objects: Book, Pen, Table, Chair, Water, Phone

Section 3: Simple Sentences & Practice (25 mins)

Build basic sentence structure.

  • "My name is..."
  • "It is a..."
  • "I like..."
  • Simple role-play based on greetings and introductions.

Section 4: Wrap-up & Review (5 mins)

Quick recap of the lesson.

  • Review new vocabulary and phrases.
  • Encourage continued practice.
